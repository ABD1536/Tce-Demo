FAQ

HOW CAN I COLLABORATE WITH TCE AS A BRAND?
Connect with our global partnerships team through our contact form. We'll help you design and execute a campaign that delivers ROI and brand impact.
WHICH PLATFORMS DO YOU COVER?
TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Snapchat, Pinterest, and emerging platforms — across 160+ markets.
DO YOU WORK WITH SMALL BUDGETS?
Yes. We work with budgets from $5k micro-campaigns all the way up to $250k+ global enterprise activations.
HOW DO YOU SELECT THE RIGHT INFLUENCERS FOR A CAMPAIGN?
We use AI-powered audience analytics, engagement quality scoring, and brand-fit matching to shortlist only the most relevant creators.
WHAT TYPES OF INFLUENCERS DO YOU WORK WITH?
Nano, Micro, Mid-tier, Macro, and Celebrity creators across all verticals — from tech and finance to fashion and lifestyle.
CAN CAMPAIGNS BE CUSTOMIZED FOR MY BRAND?
Absolutely. Every campaign we create is fully tailored to your brand's goals, audience, and voice. From influencer selection and content style to platforms and messaging, we design strategies that align with your objectives—whether it's awareness, engagement, or conversions.
DO YOU HELP WITH CONTENT STRATEGY AND CREATIVE DIRECTION?
Yes. Our creative team develops full content briefs, hooks, storyboards, and post-production quality reviews for every campaign.
HOW DO YOU MEASURE ROI FROM INFLUENCER CAMPAIGNS?
We track EMV, ROAS, CPE, CPM, traffic attribution, and conversion data through our real-time analytics dashboard.
WHY SHOULD I CHOOSE AN AGENCY INSTEAD OF MANAGING INFLUENCERS IN-HOUSE?
TCE gives you immediate access to 1M+ vetted creators, legal contracts, fraud detection, and a full campaign team — for a fraction of in-house costs.
HOW DO YOU ENSURE CAMPAIGNS RUN SMOOTHLY FROM START TO FINISH?
A dedicated account manager oversees every milestone — from creator outreach and contracts to content approvals and final reporting.
