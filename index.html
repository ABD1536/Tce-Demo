◆ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

GOT QUESTIONS? WE HAVE ANSWERS.

Everything you need to know about our creator network, performance attribution, and campaign execution.

WHAT IS INFLUENCER MANAGEMENT AND WHAT DOES AN INFLUENCER MANAGEMENT AGENCY DO?

Influencer management is the end-to-end support system behind a creator's career. It covers brand partnerships, negotiation, strategy, compliance, and long-term growth planning, so creators can focus on content while we handle the business of turning attention into sustainable income.

An influencer management agency supports creators with brand partnerships, negotiations, contracts, career development, and content strategy, handling admin so influencers focus on creativity, while maximizing growth and long-term commercial success.

HOW DOES TCE GUARANTEE HIGH-PERFORMING CREATOR MATCHES?

We leverage AI-driven audience analytics, engagement fraud auditing, and past conversion benchmarks across 1M+ vetted creators to match your brand with talent proven to convert your target demographic.

HOW FAST CAN A CAMPAIGN LAUNCH FROM BRIEF TO GO-LIVE?

Our turnkey agency infrastructure enables rapid 24-hour campaign launches. From initial brief and creator matchmaking to contract execution and content approvals, we move at startup velocity.

HOW DO YOU TRACK AND MEASURE REAL-TIME CAMPAIGN ROI?

Every campaign includes a real-time ROI dashboard tracking Earned Media Value (EMV), ROAS, CPE, custom promo code conversions, and multi-touch attribution reports for complete financial transparency.

DO YOU HANDLE CREATOR CONTRACTS, USAGE RIGHTS & PAYOUTS?

Yes, 100% turnkey. We manage bulletproof legal contracts, digital rights management for paid ad usage, tax compliance, and automated global payouts across 160+ countries.

WHAT CAMPAIGN BUDGET SIZES DOES TCE WORK WITH?

We build scalable packages tailored to growth startups and enterprise brands alike, ranging from targeted $10k-$25k test activations up to multi-market $250k+ global creator campaigns.
